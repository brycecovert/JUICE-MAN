Level 1 — Version 0

The Clean Baseline

Where it all began. A pristine Pac-Man experience — pellets, ghosts, fruit, combo system. No visual effects. No particles. Just pure, unadulterated arcade action. This is the foundation upon which all future juice is built.

Level 1 — Version 1

The First Juice

The first drops of magic. Particle effects on ghost consumption. Score popups that float upward and fade. A subtle screen glow pulses with the action. The first taste of what this project will become.

Level 1 — Version 2

Fire & Passion

The game learns to feel. Flames lick the edges of the maze. Ghost chain combos trigger fire breath effects. Screen shake on big plays. Intensity scales with your combo — the longer you survive, the hotter it gets.

Level 1 — Version 3

Water & Flow

The maze becomes alive. Waves undulate across the playfield. Ghost trails leave ripples. When power pellets activate, the entire screen shimmers like underwater. A shift to something more fluid.

Level 1 — Version 4

Neon Rush

The grid comes alive. Hot pink and electric blue slash across the maze. Pac-Man leaves a neon wake. Ghost deaths explode into laser lines. The game enters a cyberpunk fever dream.

Level 1 — Version 5

Rainbow Explosion

The game embraces chaos. Color cycles through the entire spectrum. Every action creates rainbow bursts. The HUD itself pulses with prismatic light. This is what maximum juice looks like — overwhelming, glorious, and absolutely unhinged.

Level 1 — Version 6

Dragon Mode

The dragon awakens. The maze becomes a volcanic hellscape. Fire rains from above. Ghost chains trigger increasingly apocalyptic words — "DRAGON ASCENDANT", "WORLDBURNER", "THE ETERNAL FLAME". This is not a game anymore. This is a legend.

Level 1 — Version 7

Electric Shock

Voltage multiplied. Lightning crackles across the maze. Ghost deaths create Tesla coil arcs. The power mode is a lightning storm. Every pellet crackles with energy. Touch this and you'll feel it.

Level 1 — Version 8

Cosmic Chaos

The game transcends reality. Stars swirl in the background. Pac-Man bends space around itself. Ghosts orbit like planets. The score counter uses mathematical notation for large numbers. You are not playing a game. You are touching the universe.

THE FINAL FORM

The ceiling shatters. Every effect from every version before activates simultaneously. Screen tears. Colors bleed. The game no longer respects its own boundaries. This is the end of the level 1 journey. This is what maximum juice means. The only way forward is a new level entirely.
