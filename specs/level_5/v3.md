# Level 5 — v3

The mercurial environment reacts to the sudden surge of energy from consuming a ghost, momentarily shifting from fluid silver to a dark, magnetic ferrofluid state.

- **Ferrofluid Spike-Burst**: Upon eating a ghost, the silver liquid metal at the point of impact instantly transforms into a cluster of sharp, high-contrast black ferrofluid spikes that erupt outward in a jagged, organic pattern.
- **Magnetic Resonance-Pulse**: These spikes vibrate violently with a high-frequency \"shiver\" animation, sending a dark, concentric shockwave through the surrounding chrome walls that momentarily turns them obsidian.
- **Viscous Collapse**: The spikes then melt rapidly, collapsing back into silver mercury with a heavy, liquid \"plop\" sound and a lingering, dark-to-silver gradient fade.
