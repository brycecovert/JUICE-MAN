# Level 5 — v1

The liquid world reacts violently to the consumption of its inhabitants, turning a moment of victory into a visceral, metallic explosion.

- **Mercury Bead-Collapse**: When a ghost is eaten, it instantly collapses into a single, high-reflectivity mercury bead that lingers for 100ms.
- **Symmetric Chrome-Shatter**: The bead explodes into a high-velocity, 4-way symmetric ring of liquid chrome shards that ripple outward, momentarily "painting" the maze walls in brilliant silver.
- **Surface-Tension Snap-Back**: As the shards vanish, they "snap" back toward the center of the maze in a single, high-frequency, symmetric, inward-pulling ripple, creating a visceral sense of liquid elasticity.
