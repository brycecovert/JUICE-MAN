# Level 5 — v2

The mercurial world undergoes a sudden phase transition, where the fluid elegance of liquid metal momentarily freezes into a rigid, prismatic crystalline structure.

- **Phase-Shift Crystallization**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, the flowing chrome walls instantly crystallize into high-facet, mirrored diamond structures, shifting the visual language from fluid curves to sharp, geometric edges.
- **Prismatic Refraction-Burst**: As the walls crystallize, a 4-way symmetric burst of refracted light beams shoots across the maze, splitting the viewport into a kaleidoscope of brilliant, rainbow-edged shards.
- **Molten Re-Liquefaction**: As the Power Pellet effect expires, the crystals violently "melt" back into liquid metal with a shimmering, downward-flowing animation, leaving a lingering, golden-chrome glow on the walls.
