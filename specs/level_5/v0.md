# Level 5 — v0

The Mercurial Flux: The maze is transformed into a shimmering, reflective world of flowing liquid metal, where every movement creates ripples and every impact causes molten splashes of chrome.

- **Flowing Chrome Walls**: Maze walls are composed of undulating, high-reflectivity liquid metal that ripples and shifts like a river of mercury, reflecting a dynamic, unseen environment.
- **Mercurial Surface Tension**: Pac-Man and ghosts leave a trailing "wake" of liquid metal, with shimmering surface-tension beads that snap and merge behind them as they move.
- **Molten Impact-Burst**: Consuming a pellet triggers a high-velocity splash of liquid chrome droplets that spray outward, orbit the character briefly, and then splash onto the floor with a metallic shimmer.
