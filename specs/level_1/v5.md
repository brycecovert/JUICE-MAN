# Level 1 — v5

A high-energy explosion of comic-book style neon onomatopoeia that punctuates every action with vibrant, screaming text.

- **Action Text**: Every pellet eaten triggers a tiny, fleeting "pop!" text in a random neon color at the pellet's location, scaling up and fading rapidly.
- **Ghost Chomp**: Eating a ghost triggers a massive, screen-shaking "CHOMP!" text in rainbow-cycling neon letters that occupies the center of the screen for 300ms.
- **Power Surge**: Eating a power pellet triggers a bold "POWER UP!" text that arcs across the screen in a neon electric blue glow, pulsing with the shockwave.
