# Level 1 — v2

High-energy prismatic explosions and trails that amplify the rainbow theme.

- Spectrum Trails: Pac-Man leaves a vibrant, fading rainbow trail that tracks his movement.
- Prismatic Shockwave: Eating a power pellet triggers a full-screen rainbow shockwave that ripples outward, momentarily distorting the maze.
- Chromatic Confetti: Eating a ghost triggers an explosion of high-velocity rainbow confetti particles that bounce off walls before fading.
