# Level 1 — v6

The entire playfield slowly rotates around its center, gaining speed with every pellet eaten and reversing direction each time a ghost is chomped — a dizzying, disorienting spiral.

- **Playfield Rotation**: The maze, Pac-Man, ghosts, pellets, power pellets, fruit, and all visual effects rotate as a single unit around the playfield's center. Rotation is continuous in one direction.
- **Acceleration Per Pellet**: Each pellet eaten increases rotation speed by a small increment. Starting from near-zero, the spin gradually builds from a subtle drift to a disorienting whirl by late game.
- **Ghost Chomp Reversal**: When a ghost is eaten, the rotation direction instantly reverses, creating a jarring pivot that amplifies the chaos of each chomp.
- **Fixed HUD**: Score, lives, level indicator, and overlay text remain fixed in screen space — only the playfield rotates beneath them.
- **All Sprites Rotate**: Pac-Man's mouth, ghost bodies, pellets, wall blocks, trails, particles, comic text, confetti, streamers, glitter — everything attached to the playfield rotates with it.
- **Death Reset**: On death, rotation speed resets to near-zero for the next life, giving a brief moment of calm before the spin builds again.
