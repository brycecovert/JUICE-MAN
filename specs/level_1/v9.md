# Level 1 — v9

A gravitational rainbow singularity that transforms power-up moments into a high-energy vortex of consumption.

- **Singularity Pull**: Eating a power pellet triggers a 600ms gravitational event where all nearby pellets are sucked toward Pac-Man in accelerating, spiraling arcs.
- **Vortex Visuals**: During the pull, a tightening rainbow spiral emanates from Pac-Man, with "vacuum" particles streaming inward from the edges of the screen.
- **Chromatic Implosion**: The event concludes with a sharp, inward-collapsing chromatic flash that transitions into the existing power-mode shockwave, amplifying the impact.
