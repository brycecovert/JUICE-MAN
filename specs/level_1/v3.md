# Level 1 — v3

A full-screen cascading rainbow matrix that intensifies as Pac-Man devours pellets, turning the maze into a living waterfall of chromatic chaos.

- Falling Rainbow Matrix: Vertical pixelated stripes cascade downward across the entire screen like a digital waterfall, snapping to a coarse grid for a chunky retro aesthetic
- Pellet Intensity: Stripes start at a gentle baseline speed and brightness; each pellet collected increases falling velocity and color saturation, building to overwhelming chromatic chaos at full pellet count
- Chromatic Rain: Each column generates a pseudo-random falling speed and cycles through rainbow hues via time-shifted cosine palette with red/green/blue offsets
- Glowing Caps: Bright neon orbs glow at the leading edges of falling stripes, leaving luminous trails as they descend
- Screen Vignette: Soft exponential fade darkens the top and bottom edges, keeping focus on the maze center while adding cinematic depth
- Ghost Aura: Ghosts subtly reflect the current rainbow colors washing over them
- Power Surge: Power pellet activation momentarily doubles the cascade speed and spawns reverse-flowing upward stripes
