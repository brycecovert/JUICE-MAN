# Level 1 — v3

High-frequency tactile feedback through explosive pellet consumption, turning every point gained into a mini-celebration.

- Rainbow Pellet Pops: Eating a standard pellet triggers a burst of 4-6 tiny, high-velocity neon sparks that shoot outward in random directions.
- Kinetic Sparks: Each spark inherits a random color from the rainbow palette and bounces once off the nearest wall before fading out rapidly.
- Audio-Visual Sync: Each pop is accompanied by a high-pitched, crystalline "tink" sound that varies slightly in pitch.
