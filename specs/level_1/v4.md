# Level 1 — v4

PINATA EXPLOSION EXTRAVAGANZA: The maze transforms into a celebration of chaos with candy-colored mayhem and ramped-up difficulty.

**Visual Juice:**
- Spinning Streamers: Animated rainbow paper streamers spiral continuously from each maze corner, rotating and undulating across the screen with sine-wave motion
- Glitter Storm: Constant shower of metallic glitter particles (gold, silver, hot pink, electric blue) rain down and bounce off walls with sparkle trails
- Candy Burst Pellets: Each pellet eaten explodes into 8-12 candy pieces (wrapped sweets, lollipops, gumballs) that spin outward before dissolving
- Confetti Cannon Walls: Every 3 seconds, random wall segments fire confetti bursts perpendicular to their surface
- Disco Ball Ghosts: Ghosts gain rotating faceted surfaces that reflect rainbow light beams in all directions as they move
- Chromatic Pulse Floor: Path tiles pulse with traveling rainbow waves that ripple from Pac-Man's position
- Pinata Shake: Screen wobbles with elastic bounce physics whenever a ghost is eaten (intensity scales with combo)
- Extreme Anaglyph 3D: During power mode, Pac-Man displays intense multi-layered chromatic aberration with red, blue, cyan, and green ghost layers at 8-pixel offset with animated wobble
- Profile Angry Eye: During power mode, Pac-Man shows a single large eye on the side of his face in his movement direction, with red iris, black pupil, and angled angry eyebrow sloping downward

**Difficulty Amplification:**
- Ghost Speed Boost: All ghosts move 25% faster than current speed
- Aggressive AI: Ghosts predict Pac-Man's position 5 tiles ahead instead of targeting current position
- Power Pellet Nerf: Power mode duration reduced by 30%
- Combo Pressure: Combo timer reduced by 40%, forcing faster ghost-eating chains
- Visibility Challenge: The glitter storm and streamer effects create visual noise that obscures pellets and ghost positions
