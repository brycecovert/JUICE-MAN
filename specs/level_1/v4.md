# Level 1 — v4

A prismatic haunting that turns the ghosts into trailing spectral entities, filling the maze with ghostly rainbow echoes.

- Spectral Ghost Echoes: Ghosts leave a sequence of fading, translucent silhouettes that track their precise movement path, creating a "motion blur" of ghostly forms.
- Chromatic Gradient: Each echo silhouette cycles through the rainbow palette, resulting in a vibrant, shifting rainbow trail that follows every ghost.
- Echo-Spark Feedback: When Pac-Man intersects a ghost echo, a small, high-velocity spark of that echo's color is emitted, adding tactile feedback to near-misses.
