# Level 1 — v8 (this is my intern edition)

**Onboarding Fog**
The maze begins covered in a dark semi-transparent fog of war, the intern doesn't know where anything is yet. Fog clears permanently in a smooth radius around Pac-Man as he explores. Cleared areas never re-fog. By the end of a board the whole maze is revealed, but a fresh life on a new board starts fogged again.

**Coffee Meter**
A coffee cup icon lives in the HUD. It fills incrementally with each pellet eaten. When full, Pac-Man enters OVERCAFFEINATED MODE: he moves at erratic high speed and leaves frantic zigzag light trails behind him. The cup depletes over ~8 seconds, then resets to empty.

**Slack Notification Pings**
Each time a ghost is eaten, a Slack-style notification bubble floats up from the ghost's position and fades out over ~1 second. Messages cycle through: `"great work!"`, `"crushing it"`, `"so much value"`, `"big impact"`, `"let's sync!"`, `"per my last email"`. Rendered in a rounded rectangle with a soft green glow.

**First Day Panic**
On death, instead of immediately playing the standard death animation, the screen flashes and large panicked red text appears center-screen for 400ms before the death sequence continues. Messages cycle randomly: `"reply all??"`, `"wrong Slack channel"`, `"sent to entire company"`, `"is this billable?"`.
