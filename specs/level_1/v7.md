# Level 1 — v7
Colorful bouncy balls tumble across the maze as a purely decorative overlay. All effects from v6 — playfield rotation, pellet acceleration, ghost chomp reversal, fixed HUD, sprite rotation — remain exactly as they are. Zero changes to existing behavior.
- **Matter.js**: use CDN (`https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/matter-js/0.20.0/matter.js`). Run a separate physics world for decorative balls.
- **Ball Spawning**: No balls at start. Each pellet eaten causes four new balls explode outward from that position, with a random rainbow color per pellet.  Balls are added, never removed.
- **Bouncy Balls**: Medium circles (radius ~5 px). They bounce off walls and each other purely from physics — no relation to anything else. Balls use `restitution: 0.35` so they are only bouncy. Ball physics: spawn velocity 7–15 px/s, a soft puff, `frictionAir: 0.03` so they slow down and settle, and gravity at one-third Earth (`scale: 0.0003`) so they tumble and roll rather than falling fast.
- **Fixed Gravity, Rotating Walls**: Gravity is one-third Earth's gravity (`scale: 0.0003`) and always points straight down in screen space. The maze's collision bodies rotate with the playfield. As walls tilt and turn, balls roll over the angled wall surfaces and fall down. The "down" direction is constant throughout. Balls are contained in playfield. Wall boundaries extend beyond the visible maze edges to prevent any ball from escaping.
- **Collision Layout**: Build static collision bodies from the maze grid. Balls bounce off walls but pass through open corridors freely.
- **Rendering Order (CRITICAL)**: The playfield is drawn inside a canvas rotation transform. The balls' physics positions are already in screen-space (the physics wall positions are explicitly rotated in the update loop). Therefore, balls must be drawn **after** `ctx.restore()` — outside the rotation transform. 
- **Non-Interactive**: Balls are purely decorative — they do not affect gameplay.
