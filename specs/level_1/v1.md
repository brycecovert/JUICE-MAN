# Level 1 — v1

Ghosts emit directional aiming lasers from their faces — colored to match each ghost, wrapped in spiraling white energy lines, pulsing with intensity.

- Each ghost projects a laser beam from its face in the direction it is facing, matching its signature color (Blinky=red, Pinky=pink, Inky=cyan, Clyde=orange)
- Laser beams extend until they strike a maze wall, where a small bright spark flashes at the impact point
- White spiral energy lines coil around each beam, rotating along the beam's length for a charged plasma aesthetic
- Beams pulse rhythmically — brightness oscillates between 60% and 100% at ~3Hz, giving a living "breathing" quality
- When a ghost changes direction, its laser sweeps smoothly to the new angle with a brief bright flash at the pivot
- Beam cores are thin and bright (~2px), surrounded by a soft colored glow halo (~6px) for depth
- During frightened mode, ghost lasers become erratic: rapid color-flicker between the ghost's color and white, beam wobbles side-to-side, and pulse rate doubles
- Laser emission point at each ghost's face has a small glowing nozzle effect — a bright dot that flares on each pulse peak
- Beam tips that hit walls leave a faint afterglow that fades over ~400ms, creating ghostly wall traces as ghosts patrol
