# Level 1 — v1

Rainbow color theme for walls, pellets, and ghosts.

- Walls cycle through rainbow hues using hsla() with globalTime
- Pellets glow with matching rainbow color per quadrant
- Ghosts retain their classic colors but add subtle chromatic trails
- Power pellets pulse through full spectrum
- All rainbow effects respect dt timing and MAX_PARTICLES budget
- Screen tint shifts gently across the color wheel during gameplay
- Combo counter displays in gradient rainbow text
