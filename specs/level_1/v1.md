# Level 1 — v1

Maze walls transform from blue squares into connected neon line segments with independently flashing random color fills — creating a chaotic, vibrant maze that pulses with energy.

- Maze walls redrawn as connected line segments instead of individual blue squares — shared edges between adjacent wall cells draw a single neon outline, creating a continuous maze structure
- Outline lines glow in bright neon colors cycling through the rainbow palette, giving the maze a vivid electric aesthetic
- Each enclosed wall area fills with a random bright color that flashes chaotically at 100-300ms intervals
- Every wall cell flashes independently with its own random color and timing, creating an organic, unpredictable mosaic effect
- Color transitions include a quick fade (50-100ms) between flashes for smooth but energetic visual flow
- Fill colors draw from a vibrant palette of saturated hues — electric blues, hot pinks, lime greens, oranges, purples, and yellows
- The flashing creates a living, breathing maze that feels electric and alive
- Wall outline thickness matches the existing maze scale, maintaining readability and gameplay clarity
- Existing wall collision behavior unchanged — only visual presentation transforms
