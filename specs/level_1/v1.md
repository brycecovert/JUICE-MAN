# Level 1 — v1

Transform the entire game into an over-the-shoulder 3D perspective using manual canvas-based 3D projection. The maze becomes a volumetric environment viewed from a semi-fixed camera positioned behind and above Juiceman with a slight tilt, creating depth without losing gameplay clarity.

**Juiceman:** Now a translucent golden-orange liquid globe — a sphere filled with animated juice that ripples as he moves and gurgles when eating. Mouth is rendered as an animated wedge cut through the sphere. Specular highlights give it wet, juicy reflections. Pulse animation on each pellet consumed.

**Maze Walls:** Classic blue walls extruded to 3D blocks with visible depth. Top faces are lighter blue, front faces mid-blue, side faces darker — simple flat-shaded lighting creates convincing depth. Block edges have subtle crisp lines for definition.

**Ghosts:** Volumetricized classic ghost forms. Flat bottom extrusion rising to a wavy top edge. Colored 3D bodies with glowing eyes rendered as bright white spheres on the front face. Fear mode ghosts remain functional but appear as watery translucent versions in 3D.

**Pellets & Power Pellets:** Small 3D spheres scattered across the floor plane. Regular pellets are tiny dots (rendered as small circles with subtle shading). Power pellets are larger pulsing orbs that glow intensely, growing and shrinking rhythmically. When eaten, burst into a short sparkle particle effect projected in 3D space.

**Camera Effects:** Fixed-angle over-the-shoulder perspective with slight sway toward Juiceman's position. Sharp camera shake when power pellets are consumed (0.3s) and when ghosts are eaten (0.2s). Camera briefly pulls back on ghost death explosions.

Floor plane is dark navy, providing contrast for all 3D elements. Ghost portal area uses a raised threshold with a shimmer effect at the top edge.
