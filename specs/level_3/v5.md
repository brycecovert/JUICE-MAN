# Level 3 — v5

The dream's fluid nature evolves into a sweeping, celestial vortex, where every particle of light is caught in a grand, symmetric dance.

- **Symmetric Prismatic Vortex**: All active particles (shards, rainbow trails, and dust) are now subject to a global, swirling centrifugal force that spirals them toward the center of each kaleidoscopic quadrant.
- **Velocity-Driven Turbulence**: The vortex's intensity scales with Pac-Man's speed; rapid movements create "wind shears" that whip the rainbow trails into tight, spinning spirals.
- **Harmonic Centripetal Pulse**: Periodically, the vortex reverses direction with a symmetric, rhythmic pulse, causing all screen-space debris to collapse inward and then explode outward in a coordinated wave of color.
