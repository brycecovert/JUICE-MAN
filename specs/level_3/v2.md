# Level 3 — v2

The dream's stability fractures, creating a symmetric, kaleidoscopic "tear" in the game's fabric that momentarily exposes the prismatic void beneath.

- **Symmetric Reality-Tear**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, the screen splits into four symmetric quadrants that momentarily slide apart, revealing a high-contrast, rainbow-shifted "void" behind the maze.
- **Kaleidoscopic Snap-Back**: The quadrants snap back together with a violent, symmetric jolt, releasing a ring of prismatic geometric shards that expand to the edges of the screen.
- **Chromatic Ghost-Trails**: While the "tear" is active, ghosts leave shimmering, mirrored trails of geometric echoes that vibrate in sync with the music.
