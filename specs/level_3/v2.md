# Level 3 — v2

The dream's geometry becomes unstable, momentarily warping the very fabric of the maze into a multidimensional prism.

- **Prismatic Perspective Tilt**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, the screen performs a rapid, 3D-like tilt and zoom, transforming the 2D maze into a prismatic, isometric crystal structure for 200ms.
- **Chromatic Edge-Bleed**: During the tilt, the edges of the maze walls bleed vibrant cyan and magenta, creating a high-contrast chromatic aberration effect.
- **Symmetric Snap-Back**: The world snaps back to 2D with a symmetric, outward-rippling shockwave of geometric shards that clear the screen.
