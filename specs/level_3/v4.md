# Level 3 — v4

The dream's fragmentation reaches a critical point, where every ghost-hunt ends in a gravitational collapse and a symmetric explosion of pure light.

- **Singularity Pull**: Eating a ghost creates a momentary gravitational well, violently pulling all existing rainbow trails and screen-fracture shards toward the point of impact for 150ms.
- **Prismatic Collapse**: The impact point collapses into a blinding, white-hot singularity that briefly distorts the surrounding maze walls with a heavy fish-eye lens effect.
- **Symmetric Supernova**: The singularity erupts into a massive, 4-way symmetric supernova of expanding rainbow rings and geometric shards, washing the screen in a wave of prismatic light.
