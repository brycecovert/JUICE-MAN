# Level 3 — v0

The Kaleidoscopic Dream: The game transcends physical and digital reality, entering a psychedelic, symmetric dreamscape where color and form are fluid and reactive.

- **Prismatic Symmetry**: The entire viewport is mirrored across both the X and Y axes (forming a 4-way kaleidoscopic symmetry), making every movement a complex, geometric dance of color.
- **Chroma-Shift Trails**: Pac-Man and ghosts leave long, flowing ribbons of shifting rainbow colors that curve and swirl based on their velocity, creating a vivid, painterly tapestry of movement.
- **Saturating Pulse**: Eating a power pellet triggers a "color explosion"—a radial wave of extreme saturation and hue-shifting that washes over the screen, momentarily turning the maze into a blinding, neon kaleidoscope.
