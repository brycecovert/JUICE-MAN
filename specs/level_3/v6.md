# Level 3 — v6

The dream's fluidity manifests as temporal echoes, where Pac-Man's movement leaves a trail of prismatic ghosts that shatter into geometric light.

- **Prismatic After-Images**: Pac-Man leaves a series of translucent, color-shifting after-images that linger briefly, mirroring his shape across the kaleidoscopic axes.
- **Symmetric Drift & Decay**: These echoes slowly drift outward from the center of the screen, their colors cycling through the rainbow palette as they fade.
- **Geometric Echo-Shatter**: Upon fading, each after-image erupts into a small, symmetric burst of rotating geometric shards, adding a layer of sparkling debris to the celestial vortex.
