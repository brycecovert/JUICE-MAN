# Level 3 — v1

The Dream intensifies as the laws of geometry collide, turning every victory into a burst of prismatic energy.

- **Kaleidoscopic Bloom**: Eating a ghost triggers a burst of expanding, rotating geometric shards (triangles and hexagons) that erupt from the point of impact.
- **Symmetry-Synced Eruption**: The bloom is perfectly mirrored across the X and Y axes, creating a massive, symmetric floral pattern of light that fills the viewport.
- **Chromatic Decay**: Shards rapidly cycle through the rainbow palette as they expand, eventually fading into sparkling dust that lingers for a moment.
