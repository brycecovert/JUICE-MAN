# Level 3 — v8

The dream's fragmentation culminates in a series of high-energy kinetic bursts, where every ghost-hunt generates a symmetric shockwave that ripples through the kaleidoscopic fabric.

- **Impact Time-Dilation**: Eating a ghost triggers a momentary 50ms "freeze-frame" of the entire game state, amplifying the perceived weight of the collision.
- **Symmetric Prismatic Shockwave**: A high-contrast, rainbow-shifted ring of light erupts from the impact point, mirroring across the X and Y axes and expanding rapidly to the edges of the viewport.
- **Kinetic Displacement**: The shockwave exerts a violent outward force, physically pushing all other ghosts and active screen-space particles away from the impact center in a coordinated, symmetric surge.
