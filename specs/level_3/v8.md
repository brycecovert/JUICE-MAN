# Level 3 — v8

The dream's colors reach a critical saturation point, where every ghost-hunt ends in a blinding, screen-wide chromatic surge that momentarily overrides the kaleidoscopic geometry.

- **Chromatic Ghost-Pop**: Upon eating a ghost, the screen instantly flashes a solid, high-luminance fill of that ghost's specific rainbow color for 60ms, creating a visceral "color-punch" effect.
- **Symmetric Color-Shatter**: The solid color flash then "shatters" from the center outward in a symmetric, radial wave, revealing the maze beneath as the color breaks into high-velocity, prismatic shards.
- **Luminance After-Image**: The shatter leaves a lingering, shimmering white outline of the 4-way symmetric ghost-collision points, which slowly dissolves into the existing celestial vortex.
