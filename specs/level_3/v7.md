# Level 3 — v7

The dream's fluid nature collapses into a momentary singularity upon the consumption of a ghost, creating a violent, symmetric implosion followed by a rainbow nova.

- **Symmetric Abyssal Vacuum**: Consuming a ghost triggers a rapid, symmetric suction effect, pulling all active after-images, particles, and the environment toward the four mirrored centers of the screen.
- **Prismatic Singularity Core**: At the peak of the suction, a tiny, blindingly bright white singularity appears at each center, momentarily distorting the surrounding colors into a tight, swirling vortex.
- **Rainbow Nova Burst**: The singularity instantly collapses and explodes into a high-velocity, screen-filling burst of rainbow-colored geometric shards that propagate outward and fade into the kaleidoscopic background.
