# Level 2 — v2

The simulation's stability continues to erode as the act of data collection itself generates volatile digital residue.

- **Binary Bloom**: Every dot consumed triggers a miniature explosion of glowing neon-green '0's and '1's that spray outward in a radial burst, briefly illuminating the surrounding maze area.
- **Bit-Flip Collision**: Hitting a maze wall triggers a momentary, high-frequency screen-shake combined with a single-frame horizontal flip of the entire viewport, simulating a critical memory addressing error.
- **Recursive HUD Glitch**: The score and lives display periodically \"echo,\" spawning two semi-transparent, offset copies of themselves in cyan and magenta that drift apart and snap back.
