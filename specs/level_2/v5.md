# Level 2 — v5

The simulation's instability reaches a critical threshold, manifesting as violent electrical surges and structural data-leaks.

- **Neural-Net Pulse**: A semi-transparent neon ripple pulses from the center of the maze every 5 seconds, momentarily brightening the ghosts and the maze walls in its path.
- **Instruction-Set Glitch**: While in a frightened state, ghosts lose their form and are replaced by flickering blocks of ASCII characters (e.g., "0xEF", "ERR") that shift and rotate rapidly.
- **Voltage Spike**: Eating a ghost triggers a high-intensity electrical arc—a jagged, bright white bolt—that leaps from the point of impact to the nearest maze wall, accompanied by a screen-wide, single-frame white flash.
