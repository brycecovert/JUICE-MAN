# Level 2 — v8

The simulation's failure transcends the digital; the physical hardware hosting the void begins to overheat and melt, introducing analog distortion and thermal instability.

- **Thermal Haze**: A shimmering, undulating heat-distortion effect permeates the screen edges, intensifying in frequency and amplitude as the player's score increases.
- **Chromatic Melt**: Movement creates "melting" color streaks; Pac-Man and ghosts leave trailing, saturated smears of their primary colors that slowly drip downward and dissipate.
- **Power-Rail Brownouts**: Random, high-frequency "brownouts" occur where the screen brightness momentarily dips and colors shift toward a dull, searing orange, accompanied by a low-frequency electrical hum.
