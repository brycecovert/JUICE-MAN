# Level 2 — v6

The simulation enters a state of total collapse, where the fundamental laws of resolution and synchronization break down in a violent, blocky descent into chaos.

- **Bit-Crush Resolution**: Eating a ghost triggers a "resolution crash"—the entire game viewport instantly drops to a blocky 64x64 grid for 0.3s, with colors bleeding, before snapping back to crisp resolution.
- **V-Sync Tear**: A jagged, horizontal "screen tear" line constantly drifts vertically across the viewport; any entity passing through the tear is momentarily offset by 10 pixels horizontally.
- **Kernel Panic Death**: Upon death, the screen is instantly replaced by a neon-green "System Error" screen (mocking a BSOD) that displays a "Dump File" of the player's session (Final Score, Dots Eaten, Ghosts Consumed) in a mono-spaced system font.
