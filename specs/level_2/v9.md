# Level 2 — v9

The simulation reaches its terminal state: a catastrophic hardware meltdown where the physical components ignite and the monitor itself begins to warp and char under the intense heat.

- **Incandescent Glow**: Maze walls transition from their base color to a searing, pulsing orange-white heat, with the intensity and pulse-rate increasing as the player's score rises.
- **Thermal Warp**: The center of the viewport visually bulges and distorts in a radial warp effect, simulating the monitor's glass melting and bending under extreme thermal pressure.
- **Catastrophic Pop**: Eating a ghost triggers a violent "capacitor pop"—a blinding white flash followed by a permanent, charred black "burn mark" at the impact site that persists for the remainder of the session.
