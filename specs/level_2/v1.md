# Level 2 — v1

The simulation begins to destabilize as Pac-Man's consumption of ghosts triggers systemic failures in the digital void.

- **System Crash Shockwaves**: Eating a ghost triggers a rapidly expanding, hollow square of neon green binary that pulses outward from the point of impact, momentarily causing a high-contrast chromatic flash.
- **Ghost Glitch-Shift**: Ghosts periodically "jitter" or teleport a few pixels in a random direction and back instantly, leaving behind a trailing, semi-transparent digital after-image.
- **Shatter-Death**: Upon death, Pac-Man doesn't simply vanish but shatters into a cloud of square, glowing pixels that tumble downward, simulating a corrupted file being deleted.
