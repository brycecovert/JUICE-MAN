# Level 2 — v0

The Digital Awakening: The game world reveals its nature as a glitching simulation, shifting from cosmic energy to a high-contrast, cybernetic digital void.

- **Binary Trails**: Pac-Man leaves a trailing stream of flickering green 0s and 1s that dissipate quickly, creating a data-stream effect.
- **Grid Glitch**: Maze walls occasionally "jitter" with momentary chromatic aberration (RGB split) and shift 1-2 pixels randomly, suggesting digital instability.
- **Code-Fall Powerups**: Eating a power pellet triggers a "Matrix rain" of falling green characters in the background that persists for the duration of the power-up.
