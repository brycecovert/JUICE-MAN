# Level 2 — v3

The simulation enters a state of systemic corruption as the digital void begins to tear, creating unstable geometry and persistent memory artifacts.

- **Corruption Bloom**: Eating a power pellet triggers a "digital rot" effect where the screen edges are consumed by rapidly mutating, multi-colored square artifacts that crawl inward, framing the action in a chaotic, glitchy border.
- **Memory Leak Shadows**: Every time Pac-Man changes direction, a semi-transparent, flickering "ghost frame" of his previous position is left behind, lingering for a moment before dissolving into a burst of binary noise.
- **Vertex Snap**: The maze walls periodically "snap" to a lower-resolution grid for 2-3 frames, causing the straight lines to momentarily jag and shift, simulating a failure in the simulation's rendering engine.
