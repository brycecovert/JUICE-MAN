# Level 2 — v4

The simulation attempts a desperate defragmentation process, causing the digital void to tear and the maze geometry to physically fracture.

- **Dimensional Rift**: Eating a ghost triggers a jagged, diagonal "tear" across the viewport for 0.2s, momentarily revealing a stark, white-on-black wireframe version of the game world beneath the corruption.
- **Heap Fragmentation**: Consuming a power pellet causes all maze walls within a 5-tile radius to shatter into floating, rotating cubic fragments that drift outward before snapping back into place.
- **VRAM Heatmaps**: Ghosts leave persistent, glowing neon trails of their movement paths that fade slowly over 10 seconds, eventually filling the maze with a luminous, overlapping tapestry of their patrol routes.
