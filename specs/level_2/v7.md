# Level 2 — v7

The simulation's hardware begins to physically fail, manifesting as catastrophic GPU artifacting and memory overflows as the digital void reaches critical instability.

- **GPU Artifacting**: High-intensity, saturated rainbow streaks flicker randomly across the screen; the frequency and duration of these streaks increase as the player's score rises.
- **Buffer Overflow**: Eating a ghost triggers a "data flood"—a torrent of scrolling hexadecimal characters that surge from the bottom to the top of the viewport for 0.5s.
- **Heap Corruption**: Ghosts leave behind a trailing wake of drifting, colorful "memory leaks" (square pixel blocks) that float upwards and dissipate, creating a persistent, smog-like digital haze in the maze.
