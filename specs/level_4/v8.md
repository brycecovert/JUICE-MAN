# Level 4 — v8

The void's gravitational influence becomes an active predator, warping the player's perception of space as the cosmic abyss exerts a crushing, claustrophobic pressure.

- **Dynamic Horizon Warp**: The viewport employs a constant, subtle fish-eye lens effect that curves the maze boundaries; the curvature intensifies dynamically as the nearest ghost approaches, creating a visceral sense of impending collapse.
- **Singularity Tunnel-Vision**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, the warp violently inverts from convex to concave, transforming the screen into a high-speed "tunnel" that pulls the visual field toward the center just before the v1 singularity implosion.
- **Gravitational Lens-Flare**: As the warp intensifies, the edges of the screen bleed a shimmering, distorted purple corona, with light from the maze walls bending around the edges of the viewport in a cosmic lensing effect.
