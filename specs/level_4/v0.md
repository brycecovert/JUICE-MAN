# Level 4 — v0

The Eldritch Void: The maze transforms into a living, breathing entity of the void, where reality bends and the hunt becomes a descent into cosmic madness.

- **Void-Pulse Background**: The background is a deep, swirling abyss of purple and black that pulses in a slow, rhythmic "breath," occasionally revealing glimpses of colossal, blinking eyes.
- **Eldritch Tendrils**: Maze walls are not solid lines but are composed of undulating, dark-purple tentacles that subtly writhe and sway, creating a sense of organic instability.
- **Sanity-Shatter Trail**: Pac-Man leaves a trail of "fractured reality"—tiny, shimmering shards of white light and distorted space that orbit his position and slowly drift away like dying stars.
