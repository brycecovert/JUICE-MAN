# Level 4 — v5

The void's patience ends, and the abyssal entity finally consumes the intruder, transforming the moment of defeat into a violent, symmetric spatial collapse.

- **Symmetric Void-Swallow**: Upon death, four massive, obsidian-black void-jaws snap inward from the four corners of the viewport, closing in on the center.
- **Spatial Squeeze-Distortion**: As the jaws close, the entire game world is violently "squeezed" toward the center point, creating a high-frequency, symmetric spatial warp.
- **The Final Snap**: The jaws collide at the center with a blinding, single-frame chromatic aberration flash, instantly snapping the screen to total blackness.
