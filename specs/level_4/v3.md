# Level 4 — v3

The void's hunger transcends momentary singularities, transforming Pac-Man himself into a sustained gravitational vortex that consumes the abyss.

- **Sustained Vortex Aura**: During Power Pellet empowerment, Pac-Man is enveloped in a high-density, swirling vortex of dark-purple energy and orbiting void-shards.
- **Abyssal Gravitational Pull**: A constant gravitational force pulls nearby pellets and environment particles toward Pac-Man, creating a visceral "vacuum" effect.
- **Vacuum-Siphon Distortion**: The area immediately surrounding Pac-Man experiences an intense, oscillating radial blur and chromatic aberration, visually warping the maze as he moves.
