# Level 4 — v3

The void's instability evolves into a symmetric, atmospheric storm, where the abyss breathes with electric violence.

- **Symmetric Void-Lightning**: Random, high-frequency bolts of purple-white lightning strike the maze walls in 4-way symmetry, momentarily illuminating the tendrils with blinding, high-contrast flashes.
- **Swirling Abyssal-Mists**: The background pulse evolves into a symmetric, slowly rotating vortex of deep purple and obsidian mists, creating a visceral sense of being pulled into a cosmic drain.
- **Void-Static Discharge**: Rapid changes in Pac-Man's direction trigger a symmetric burst of "void-static"—tiny, high-energy purple sparks and white-noise glitches that ripple outward from his position.
