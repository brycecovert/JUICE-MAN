# Level 4 — v3

The void is not just a place, but a presence. The background eyes cease to be passive, occasionally manifesting as a viewport-filling entity that momentarily overrides reality.

- **The Great Abyssal Blink**: Every 15 seconds, the entire game world momentarily "blinks" out of existence for 150ms, replaced by a single, colossal, hyper-detailed Eldritch Eye that fills the entire screen.
- **Symmetric Pupil-Dilate**: As the eye appears, its pupil rapidly dilates in a 4-way symmetric expansion, creating a powerful vacuum effect that pulls all current particles toward the four corners of the screen.
- **Chromatic Snap-Back**: When the eye blinks shut, the world returns with a violent "snap," accompanied by a full-screen chromatic aberration flash and a momentary inversion of all colors that rapidly fades back to normal.
