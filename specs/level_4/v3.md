# Level 4 — v3

The void's gaze becomes absolute, shifting from a distant observer to an oppressive, omniscient presence that dominates the player's perception during moments of power.

- **The Great Unblinking**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, the background abyss is instantly replaced by a single, colossal, hyper-detailed cosmic eye that fills the entire viewport.
- **Omniscient Tracking**: The pupil of the colossal eye dynamically tracks Pac-Man's position in real-time, creating an unsettling sensation of being watched by a god-like entity.
- **Pupillary Contraction**: As the Power Pellet effect expires, the eye's pupil violently contracts into a pinprick of light before "blinking" shut in a rapid, screen-wide shutter effect that returns the world to the pulsing void.
