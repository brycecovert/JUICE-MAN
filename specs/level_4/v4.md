# Level 4 — v4

The void's hunger manifests as a violent, symmetric reaction, where the act of consuming ghosts triggers a momentary spatial collapse that pinches the fabric of reality.

- **Symmetric Void-Lash**: Eating a ghost triggers four obsidian-black, high-velocity "lashes" that strike from the four corners of the viewport toward the point of impact.
- **Spatial Pinch-Distortion**: At the moment of impact, the entire game world is violently "pinched" toward the center of the cross, creating a high-frequency, symmetric spatial distortion.
- **Chromatic Resonance-Snap**: The lashes instantly vanish with a sharp, symmetric chromatic aberration flash and a momentary inversion of the void's colors that ripples outward.
