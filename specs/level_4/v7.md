# Level 4 — v7

The void's omniscience manifests as an oppressive, watchful presence, where the environment itself tracks the intruder with a thousand blinking, abyssal eyes.

- **Omnipresent Abyssal Eyes**: Dozens of small, obsidian-purple eyes manifest randomly along the maze walls, their pupils constantly tracking Pac-Man's position with a slight, organic lag.
- **Symmetric Gaze-Flare**: Upon eating a Power Pellet, all active eyes widen in a symmetric shock, emitting high-intensity, purple light-beams that illuminate the maze in 4-way symmetric corridors.
- **Blink-Shatter**: When a ghost is eaten, the nearest eyes "blink" violently, triggering a localized, symmetric burst of void-shards and a momentary, high-frequency chromatic aberration flash.
