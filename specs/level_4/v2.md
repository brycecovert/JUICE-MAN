# Level 4 — v2

The void's instability reaches a breaking point, where the act of consumption doesn't just trigger a singularity, but physically fractures the fabric of the abyssal reality.

- **Symmetric Void-Fracture**: Eating a ghost triggers a sudden, 4-way symmetric "cracking" of the viewport, where jagged, obsidian-black fissures rip across the screen.
- **Abyssal Shard-Drift**: The fractured shards of the game world momentarily slide apart by a few pixels, revealing a blinding, pulsing white-purple void beneath the cracks.
- **Reality Re-Stitch**: The cracks rapidly "zip" shut with a high-energy, chromatic flash, snapping the world back together with a violent, screen-shaking jolt.
