# Level 4 — v6

The void's instability manifests as temporal echoes, where Pac-Man's existence is mirrored across the abyssal axes in a shimmering, unstable dance of obsidian energy.

- **Symmetric Mirror-Echoes**: Pac-Man is trailed by three semi-transparent, obsidian-purple \"echoes\" that mirror his movement across the X and Y axes, creating a constant 4-way symmetric presence.
- **Echo-Shatter**: Eating a ghost triggers a violent collision of the echoes at the impact point, erupting into a symmetric burst of high-contrast void-shards and purple sparks.
- **Resonant Distortion**: As Pac-Man moves, the echoes' opacity and \"jitter\" increase based on velocity, with high-frequency chromatic aberration intensifying during fast turns.
