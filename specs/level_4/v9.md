# Level 4 — v9

The void's instability reaches its absolute zenith in a final, symmetric collapse, where the boundary between the intruder and the abyss dissolves into a blinding, kaleidoscopic apotheosis.

- **Cosmic Core-Radiance**: Pac-Man is enveloped in a constant, pulsing, 4-way symmetric aura of obsidian and gold energy that oscillates in intensity with the game's heartbeat.
- **Symmetric Void-Shatter**: Eating a ghost triggers a violent "shatter" effect where the entire viewport breaks into a kaleidoscopic grid of obsidian shards, each reflecting a mirrored, distorted version of the maze.
- **The Great Collapse**: The shards rapidly rotate and collapse inward toward Pac-Man in a high-speed, symmetric spiral, culminating in a blinding, viewport-filling white-purple flash that shatters the remaining reality.
