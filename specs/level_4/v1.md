# Level 4 — v1

The void's hunger manifests as a gravitational collapse, where the act of empowerment triggers a momentary singularity that warps the very fabric of the abyss.

- **Event-Triggered Implosion**: Eating a Power Pellet triggers a 100ms violent spiral-implosion, pulling the maze walls, ghosts, and particles toward a central singularity point.
- **Singularity Flash**: At the moment of maximum compression, a blinding, high-contrast white-purple flash erupts from the center of the viewport.
- **Cosmic Rebound**: The singularity detonates, snapping the game world back to its original state via a symmetric, outward-rippling shockwave of void-shards and chromatic distortion.
